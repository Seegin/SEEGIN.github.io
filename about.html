About关于

I am Xianzhong Liang (SEEGIN), a computer-science graduate from Xiamen University, joining the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences in fall 2026. My focus is the engineering layer between a learning algorithm and a robot that actually moves: simulation, teleoperation, data pipelines, training, rollout, and skill scheduling — with a growing interest in systems software and robotics infrastructure. 我是梁献中（SEEGIN），厦门大学计算机科学与技术本科毕业，2026 年秋季将入学中国科学院软件研究所。 我关注学习算法与真正运动起来的机器人之间的系统工程：仿真、示教、数据管线、训练、rollout 与技能调度； 方向偏底层系统软件与机器人系统支撑。
